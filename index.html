OUR STORY · আমাদের গল্প

A heritage canteen,
reborn as Vheto.

  • Once the Serampore Court compound's own canteen — red-brick walls, green shutters, high beamed ceilings.
  • Red chairs, wooden tables, ceiling fans turning slow overhead.
  • Plates that taste like they came from a thakuma's kitchen — nothing fancy, everything honest.
